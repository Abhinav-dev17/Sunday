    How to play

    WASD / arrows
    Move. Shift runs, Space jumps.
    Drag · scroll
    Orbit and zoom the camera.
    E
    Gather, enter doors, talk, use stations, farm.
    1–9
    Pick a hotbar slot. Tools and seeds act on what you face.
    Click
    With a block or furniture selected, place it on your plot or in your cottage.
    Right-click · X
    Remove a placed piece. R rotates.
    B · C · M · H
    Backpack, hand crafting, map, this panel.

    Wood comes from trees (slow by hand, fast with an axe). Rocks need a pickaxe, iron needs a stone pickaxe. Craft at a workbench, smelt at a furnace. Seeds are sold in the General Store; till soil with a hoe in Greenfield Gardens.
